## Overview
- Designed, manufactured, and constructed a 2 person canoe as part of a team using only cardboard, duct tape, and plastic bags. Final design was shaped using mathematical functions and used Autodesk to simulate fluid dynamics.
- Final canoe completed 6+ laps across the Brophy 60m competition pool without leaking and managed to accommodate 6 students at once including 2 audience members during several victory laps.
